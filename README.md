# 🤖 TCOR - Your Digital Companion
A powerful virtual assistant for your computer, designed to automate your daily tasks and boost your productivity.

## ✨ Features
- 🧠 **Track your digital habits** automatically
- 💡 **Suggest apps or websites** for you to use
- 📂 **Open apps/files** instantly
- 🌐 **Open websites** via voice or text
- 📝 **Conduct in-depth research** and generate reports (Word format)
- ⛅ **Check the weather**
- ⏰ **Set alarms**
- 📸 **Take screenshots**
- 🔒 **Lock your screen**
- 🔊 **Adjust device volume**
- 🗒️ **Take notes**

## ⚙️ Assistant Settings
- 🎙️ **Adjust the assistant's voice volume**
- 🔄 **Seamlessly switch** between chat and voice modes
- 📱 **Compact interface** (similar to Siri)
- 🚀 **Auto-launch** upon system startup
